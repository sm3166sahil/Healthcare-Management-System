Navigate to Data Cleaning. Highlight the synthetic dataset audit: missing values, duplicates, and IQR outliers. Execute imputation and duplicate removal.

Show descriptive statistics (skewness, kurtosis, IQR) and Pearson correlation matrix. Emphasize that correlation does NOT imply medical causation.
Step 5: Visualizations & PDF Reports

Open JSON & RegEx. Test pattern matching for Patient IDs (PAT-\d{4}) and emails. Demonstrate JSON export from the SQLite database.
